Privacy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. The games have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not receive any data from you.

What stays on your device

Your best scores, achievements and settings (sound, music, vibration, kids mode) are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device, so they are still there next time. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

To work offline, the browser also keeps a copy of the game files on your device.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you load the page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The page also loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

Tips

The Support section shows a public Solana address. If you choose to send a tip, the transfer happens in your own wallet app and is recorded on the public Solana blockchain. We do not see anything from the website itself.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
